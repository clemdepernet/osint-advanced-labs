# Rules of Engagement — "Who Am I?" challenge

Team name: ____________________   Date (UTC): ____________

We, the undersigned, will during this challenge:

1. Collect **passively only**: no contact with the target, their employer, colleagues or relatives, on any channel.
2. Never attempt to authenticate as the target, trigger password resets, or run probes that send anything to the target.
3. Use **free, public** sources only: no breach dumps, no paid people-search, no dark web.
4. Stop at "a family member exists": no names, photos or details of relatives or minors.
5. Stop at **city level** for any location: no street address, building, plate or home photos.
6. Source every fact with a URL we opened ourselves. AI output is a lead, never a source.
7. Stop collecting when the 45-minute timer rings.
8. **Delete** every note, download and browser trace about the target at the end of the debrief, in front of the instructor.

We understand that breaking any rule disqualifies the team and may be reported under the school's code of conduct and applicable data-protection law (GDPR art. 5, 6).
